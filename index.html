1
Morning CRM Review

Open CRM. Flag agents inactive 30+ days. Tag them Outreach Today. Pull your 10-call list before 9 AM.

10 Outreach Calls

Lead with inactive agents. Slow down. Smile before you dial. Let them talk 70% of the call. No pitch - curiosity only.

Within 30 minutes of each call. Note tone, follow-up needed, next step. Update tag in CRM immediately.
